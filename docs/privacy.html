ApplyTrail Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

ApplyTrail is a browser extension that helps you keep track of your own job applications. It has no server of its own: the developer does not receive, collect or store any of your data.

What the extension reads

  • Job posting pages you visit (company career sites, LinkedIn, Indeed, Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, Ashby and similar): the job title, company, location, salary and job description, so they can be saved when you record an application; and the "application submitted" confirmation text, so the application can be recorded automatically. On pages that are not job-related, the extension does nothing.
  • Gmail on the web (mail.google.com), while you have it open: the sender, subject and preview text of emails in the list, and the body of an email you open, to recognize recruiting emails (online assessments, interview invitations, rejections, offers and application confirmations). This happens inside your browser. Emails that are not about job applications are ignored and not stored.
  • Your Google account email address, only if you choose to sign in with Google for sync, to show which account is syncing.

What is stored, and where

  • Your application records: company, title, job description, status, dates, interview rounds and the notes you write.
  • For recognized recruiting emails: the subject, sender name and address, date, the detected type, and the sentence that triggered the recognition (at most 200 characters). The rest of the email is not stored.
  • Your settings, such as the interface language.

This data is stored in the extension's storage in your browser. If you sign in with Google, the same data is also saved as one file in the hidden app data folder of your own Google Drive, which only this extension can access, so that your other computers can sync it. The sign-in token is kept in memory for the current browser session only.

Google user data

  • Requested permissions: openid and email (your email address, shown in Settings) and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata (the sync file in the hidden app data folder). The extension cannot see any other files in your Drive.
  • Google user data is used only to sync your records between your own computers. It is not transferred to the developer or anyone else, not used for advertising, and not read by people.
  • ApplyTrail's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared or transferred to anyone. The extension uses no analytics, advertising or tracking, and loads no remote code.

Your choices

  • Edit or delete any record, or delete all data in Settings. With sync on, deletions also apply to the copy in your Google Drive.
  • Turn off automatic recording or email recognition in Settings.
  • Sign out of sync in Settings at any time. To remove the extension's access to your Google account, visit myaccount.google.com/permissions. To delete the sync file, open Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → ApplyTrail → Options → Delete hidden app data.
  • Uninstalling the extension removes all data stored in your browser.

Children

ApplyTrail is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

ApplyTrail 隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 11 月 5 日

ApplyTrail 是一个帮你记录求职投递的浏览器插件。它没有自己的服务器：开发者不会收到、收集或保存你的任何数据。

插件读取什么

  • 你访问的职位页面（公司官网、LinkedIn、Indeed、Greenhouse、Lever、Workday、Ashby 等）：职位名、公司、地点、薪资和 JD，用于记录投递时保存；以及“申请已提交”的确认文字，用于自动记录。与求职无关的页面上，插件不做任何事。
  • Gmail 网页版（mail.google.com，仅在你打开时）：列表里邮件的发件人、标题、摘要，以及你打开的那封邮件的正文，用于识别求职邮件（OA、面试邀请、拒信、Offer、投递确认）。识别在你的浏览器里完成；与求职无关的邮件直接忽略，不保存。
  • 你的 Google 账号邮箱：只在你选择用 Google 登录同步时读取，用于显示正在同步的账号。

保存什么、存在哪里

  • 你的投递记录：公司、职位、JD、状态、日期、面试轮次和你写的笔记。
  • 识别出的求职邮件：标题、发件人名字和地址、日期、识别类型，以及触发识别的那一句话（最多 200 个字符）。邮件其他内容不保存。
  • 你的设置，例如界面语言。

这些数据保存在浏览器的插件存储里。如果你用 Google 登录，同一份数据也会作为一个文件保存在你自己 Google Drive 的隐藏应用数据文件夹里（只有本插件能访问），用于在你的多台电脑之间同步。登录凭证只保存在当前浏览器会话的内存中。

Google 用户数据

  • 申请的权限：openid、email（你的邮箱地址，显示在设置里）和 https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata（隐藏应用数据文件夹里的同步文件）。插件看不到你 Drive 里的其他文件。
  • Google 用户数据只用于在你自己的电脑之间同步记录，不会传给开发者或任何人，不用于广告，也不会被人查看。
  • ApplyTrail 对通过 Google API 获得的信息的使用和传输遵守 Google API Services User Data Policy，包括其中的 Limited Use 要求。

共享

不出售、不共享、不转让任何数据。插件不使用统计、广告或跟踪，也不加载远程代码。

你可以

  • 修改或删除任何记录，或在设置里清空全部数据；开启同步时，删除也会同步到 Google Drive 里的那份。
  • 在设置里关闭自动记录或邮件识别。
  • 随时在设置里退出同步。要撤销插件对 Google 账号的访问，打开 myaccount.google.com/permissions。要删除同步文件：Google Drive → 设置 → 管理应用 → ApplyTrail → 选项 → 删除隐藏的应用数据。
  • 卸载插件会删除浏览器里保存的所有数据。

儿童

ApplyTrail 不面向 13 岁以下儿童。

变更

本政策如有变更，新版本会发布在本页并更新日期。
